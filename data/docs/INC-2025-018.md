## Summary
On 2025-08-02 customers experienced failed card and transfer payments for 128 minutes (SEV1). Payment success rate dropped below 80%.

## Timeline
- T+0: Alerting fired on payment failure rate. On-call engineer paged.
- T+10: Incident declared, payments-core and card-gateway dashboards reviewed.
- T+128: Service restored and success rate returned to normal.

## Root Cause
Expired TLS certificate on card-gateway link. The mutual-TLS certificate for the card-gateway integration expired because renewal reminders went to a decommissioned mailbox.

## Impact
Approximately 15360 failed payment attempts; no data loss. Customer support received elevated call volume.

## Remediation
Certificate rotated manually; expiry monitoring added with 30/14/7 day alerts to the on-call channel. Follow runbook RB-002.
