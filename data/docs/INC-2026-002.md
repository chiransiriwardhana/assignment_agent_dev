## Summary
On 2026-02-03 customers experienced failed card and transfer payments for 44 minutes (SEV2). Payment success rate dropped below 80%.

## Timeline
- T+0: Alerting fired on payment failure rate. On-call engineer paged.
- T+10: Incident declared, payments-core and card-gateway dashboards reviewed.
- T+44: Service restored and success rate returned to normal.

## Root Cause
Database connection pool exhaustion. payments-core exhausted its PostgreSQL connection pool after a traffic spike; client retries amplified the load.

## Impact
Approximately 5280 failed payment attempts; no data loss. Customer support received elevated call volume.

## Remediation
Pool size raised from 100 to 250, retry back-off with jitter added, pool saturation alert at 80%. Follow runbook RB-001.
