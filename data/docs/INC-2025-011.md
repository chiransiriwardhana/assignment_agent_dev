## Summary
On 2025-05-08 customers experienced failed card and transfer payments for 62 minutes (SEV2). Payment success rate dropped below 80%.

## Timeline
- T+0: Alerting fired on payment failure rate. On-call engineer paged.
- T+10: Incident declared, payments-core and card-gateway dashboards reviewed.
- T+62: Service restored and success rate returned to normal.

## Root Cause
Faulty configuration deployment. A configuration change to payment routing rules was deployed without canary validation and sent traffic to a disabled route.

## Impact
Approximately 7440 failed payment attempts; no data loss. Customer support received elevated call volume.

## Remediation
Change rolled back; mandatory canary stage and config schema validation added to the pipeline. Follow runbook RB-005.
