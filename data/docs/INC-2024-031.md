## Summary
On 2024-11-20 customers experienced failed card and transfer payments for 36 minutes (SEV3). Payment success rate dropped below 80%.

## Timeline
- T+0: Alerting fired on payment failure rate. On-call engineer paged.
- T+10: Incident declared, payments-core and card-gateway dashboards reviewed.
- T+36: Service restored and success rate returned to normal.

## Root Cause
Third-party card gateway timeouts. The external card gateway degraded and returned timeouts; payments-core had no circuit breaker so threads were held until exhaustion.

## Impact
Approximately 4320 failed payment attempts; no data loss. Customer support received elevated call volume.

## Remediation
Circuit breaker and fallback gateway routing introduced; timeout reduced from 30s to 5s. Follow runbook RB-003.
